    Paste a stat block

    Photograph the book page, copy the text (Samsung's text extraction or Google Lens), and paste it here. Everything lands in the editor so you can fix anything the photo scrambled before saving.

    Settings

    Display

    Red light drops color entirely and uses bold white-on-black so everything stays readable under your combat lighting.

    Spellcasting at the table

    Applies to every caster. Stat blocks show a Spell Slots or Spell Points box right under the Spellcasting trait, and the combat tracker (coming next) will count down whichever you pick. Spell points use your house rule for 5th-level and higher spells.

    Backup and transfer

    A backup holds every monster you've created or edited. Loading one merges it in: new monsters are added, and when the same monster exists on both devices, the most recently edited version wins. Nothing else is removed.
