    Settings

    Display

    Red light drops color entirely and uses bold white-on-black so everything stays readable under your combat lighting.

    Spellcasting at the table

    Applies to every caster. Stat blocks show a Spell Slots or Spell Points box right under the Spellcasting trait, and the combat tracker counts down whichever you pick. Spell points use your house rule for 5th-level and higher spells.

    Turn order

    Active Initiative (Giffyglyph's Darker Dungeons): no rolls; whoever finishes their turn picks who goes next. Five or more of the same monster act as one group, damaged creatures can interrupt for free, and you get one interrupt point per player each fight.

    Monster HP in combat

    Random by CR gives each monster that joins a fight its own HP from the DMG's hit point range for its challenge rating, so two of the same monster can differ. Stat blocks themselves don't change.

    Google Drive sync

    Backup and transfer

    A backup holds every monster you've created or edited, plus your party and saved encounters. Loading one merges it in: new monsters are added, and when the same monster exists on both devices, the most recently edited version wins. Nothing else is removed.
